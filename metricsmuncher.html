Metrics Muncher

Every account you use to run an app, in one dashboard. Metrics Muncher links Google Analytics, AdMob, Firebase, and Apple App Store Connect together into a single visualisation, so you can finally see the whole picture without five separate logins.

Coming Soon to the App Store

Built for Devs Tired of Tab-Switching

If you ship apps, you already know the ritual: check Google Analytics for traffic, flip to AdMob for revenue, open Firebase for crashes and engagement, then log into App Store Connect for downloads and sales. Four accounts, four logins, four mental models, and no single number that tells you how your app is actually doing today.

We built Metrics Muncher because we were living that ritual every single day for our own apps. It links your existing Google Analytics, AdMob, Firebase, and Apple App Store Connect accounts and brings the numbers that matter into one clean visualisation. No spreadsheets, no exporting CSVs, no mental math to reconcile revenue against downloads.

One Visualisation, Four Sources

Metrics Muncher doesn't try to replace any of these platforms, it sits on top of them. Behind the scenes, it talks to each service's own reporting APIs using credentials you control, then lines the results up on shared timelines so you can spot the connections a single dashboard would otherwise hide, like a spike in AdMob revenue that lines up with an App Store featuring, or a Firebase crash that quietly tanked your Analytics engagement the same week.

  • Google Analytics: Traffic, sessions, and user behaviour, pulled straight from your existing property.
  • AdMob: Ad revenue, eCPM, and fill rate trends alongside the rest of your metrics.
  • Firebase: Crashes, engagement, and retention data in the same view as your revenue.
  • Apple App Store Connect: Downloads, proceeds, and territory breakdowns without opening a browser.
  • Combined Trends: Cross-reference every source on shared timelines to spot cause and effect.
  • Built for Indies: Designed around the workflow of a solo developer or small team, not an enterprise BI team.
  • No Spreadsheets: Skip the manual CSV exports and copy-pasting between tools.
  • Your Accounts, Your Control: Metrics Muncher connects to accounts you already own using official APIs.

Why We Built It

Tayloriellos runs several apps across health, finance, travel, food, and games, each with its own Analytics property, AdMob unit, Firebase project, and App Store Connect listing. Multiply that by six apps and checking performance became a part-time job in itself. Metrics Muncher is the tool we wished existed: the missing dashboard layer over the accounts every indie developer already has open in a dozen browser tabs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accounts does Metrics Muncher connect to?

Metrics Muncher links Google Analytics, AdMob, Firebase, and Apple App Store Connect. Sign in to each account once, and Metrics Muncher pulls the numbers that matter into a single combined view.

Is Metrics Muncher only for iOS developers?

Metrics Muncher is built as an iOS app so you can check your metrics from your phone, but it's aimed at any developer shipping apps through the App Store who also relies on Google Analytics, AdMob, or Firebase to understand how those apps are performing.

Does Metrics Muncher store my analytics data on a server?

Metrics Muncher connects directly to the official APIs for each service using credentials you control. We don't build a shadow copy of your business data on our own servers beyond what's needed to render your dashboard. See our privacy policy for the full details.

How much does Metrics Muncher cost?

Metrics Muncher is free to download. As with our other apps, there are no forced accounts and no hidden subscriptions to view your own data.
