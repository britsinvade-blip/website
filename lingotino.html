Lingotino

Learn Italian properly, from your first ciao to C2. Lingotino is a complete course for iPhone that teaches you something before it tests you, gets you speaking out loud from day one, and prepares you for the CILS B1 Cittadinanza exam.

Taught First, Then Tested

Most language apps quiz you on words you've never seen. Lingotino doesn't. Every new word, verb, phrase or grammar rule appears first on a learn card, with audio, before any question asks for it. Then you climb a ladder: recognise it, recall it, then produce it yourself. Get something wrong and it comes back at the end of the session, and again in your reviews until it sticks.

A Real Course, A1 to C2

Lingotino is built around units and lessons, the way a good teacher would plan a course, with thousands of words and hundreds of verbs across all six levels. A short placement test finds your starting point, and the app's own instructions move gradually into Italian as you progress.

  • Daily Sessions: A short mix of review and new material, paced by spaced repetition.
  • Speaking & Pronunciation: Repeat, translate and answer out loud, with word-by-word feedback.
  • Listening & Reading: Recordings and texts at your level, with exam-style questions.
  • AI Conversation Partner: Practise real situations, from ordering at a restaurant to calling about a flat to rent, with a partner powered by Apple Intelligence.
  • Writing Feedback: Write short texts and get marks and corrections on task, grammar and vocabulary.
  • Verb Tables & Grammar Help: Every tense, and grammar terms explained in plain English.
  • Weak Areas: Lingotino spots the words, verbs and tenses you struggle with and trains them.
  • Culture: Notes on everyday Italian life at every level.

Ready for CILS B1 Cittadinanza

If you're applying for Italian citizenship, Lingotino has full CILS B1 Cittadinanza mock exams in the format of the official papers: listening, reading, writing and a spoken test with an AI examiner, marked on the official criteria. A checkpoint at the end of every level tells you when you're ready to move up.

Private by Design

No account, no email, no sign-up. Your progress lives on your iPhone and syncs only through your own iCloud. Lingotino sends anonymous usage data so we can improve the questions learners find hardest, never your name or your own words, and you can turn it off in Settings. Frequently Asked Questions

What level of Italian is Lingotino for?

Every level, from complete beginner (A1) to mastery (C2). A short placement test puts you at the right level, and you can change it at any time.

Can Lingotino help me pass the CILS B1 Cittadinanza exam?

Yes. Lingotino includes full CILS B1 Cittadinanza mock exams in the format of the official papers, covering listening, reading, writing and a spoken test with an AI examiner, plus checkpoints at the end of each level.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is stored on your iPhone and synced through your own private iCloud account. We don't run a server that holds your learning data.

How much does Lingotino cost?

Lingotino is completely free.
