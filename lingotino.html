Lingotino

Learn Italian from zero to fluency. Lingotino is a complete Italian course, A1 to C2, that teaches like a great classroom: clear lessons, real speaking practice, conversations with an AI partner and a certificate at every level. Tino the monkey keeps you going, a few minutes a day.

Lingotino at a Glance

  • The course: six CEFR levels (A1 to C2), about 50 units, 355 lessons, 5,400 words and 950 verbs.
  • Every skill: 308 recordings, 98 reading texts, speaking practice in every unit and writing tasks from A2.
  • Tino, your AI partner: 21 real-life role-plays, a teacher's note after every activity and marked writing, powered by Apple Intelligence.
  • Exams: a placement test, a certificate exam at every level and dedicated CILS B1 Cittadinanza preparation.
  • Private: no account; progress syncs through your own iCloud.

Taught First, Then Tested

Lingotino works the way good language teachers do: explain, practise, then use it for real. Every grammar idea is explained in plain English before you practise it, nothing is tested before it's taught, and each new word climbs a ladder from recognising it to saying it yourself. As you progress, the app itself moves into Italian, from English at A1 to all Italian from B2.
